Precision@K
- Precision@k = (Relevant items in top k) / k
- Top 5 recommended → how many of them were good?
- [Charizard, Lapras, Snorax, Pikachu, Gengar] → useful 
[charizar & gengar] → precision@5 = ⅖ =0.4
- 2 out of 5 were good.
- From the recommended items, how much were good?



--- PAGE 14 ---
Recall@K
- Recall@k = (Relevant items in top k) / (total relevant items)
- How many relevant stuff was existing? 
- [Charizard, Blaziken, Gengar] → useful [charizar & gengar] 
→ precision@5 = 2/3 =0.667
- 2 out of 3 were predicted.
- How many from the good ones did we find?

HitRate@K
- HitRate@K –= [1 if at least one item is relevant in our top-k else 0]
- Top-5 has Charizard → HitRate@5 = 1
- Top5 has Charizard and Blaziken → hitRate@5 = 1
- Very used by netflix and YouTube since it is nice ones we have one good item at least. So it does love 
Finding at least one good.


--- PAGE 18 ---
MAP (Mean Average Precision)
- Averages the precision for each relevant position.
- We are awarding that relevant items are ranked higher.
- The formula:
- MAP = (1/|R|)*SUM(Precision@K)
- Where R is the total number of relevant items
- Using the same example as before:
- relevant items = [Charizard, Blaziken, Gengar] we predict → [Lapras, Charizard, Gengar, Hitmochan, 
Magikarp Blaziken] → What’s the value?


--- PAGE 19 ---
NDCG (Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain)
- (Seen two classes ago= where DCG@K is:
- Where rel_i is the relevance of an item at position i.
- It penalizes that good items are down.
- IDCG → Perfect ranking.
- NDCG@k = (DCG@K) / (IDCG@K)
- Charizard → 3 | Lapras → 2 | Gengar → 1 | Snorlax → 0|
- 3 = Amazing | 2 = Nice | 1 = ok | 0 = FATAL
- We predict [Laptras, Charizard, Snorlax, Gengar] 
■ POS 1 = [2/log_2(2) = 2/ 1 = 2
■ POS2 = 3/log_2(3) = 1.89
■ POS3 = 0/log_2(4) = 0
■ POS4 = 1/log_2(5) = 0.43
■ DCG = 2+1.89+0+0.43 = 4.32
■ IDCG = 3 + 1.26 + 0.5 + 0 = 4.76
- 4.32/4.76 = 0.91 nice
